About the Program

Overview Of Family Business Management Course

AAA Learning Model 1 Week / Month on Campus Succession Planning Mumbai Campus

WeSchool's PGP in Entrepreneurship and Family Managed Business (EFMB) is India's leading entrepreneurship course in Mumbai that addresses the unique challenges of family enterprises through practical, immersive learning.

What makes this program unique:

  • Structured on the AAA Model (Acquisition, Application & Assimilation of Knowledge) across two semesters over 11 months
  • Addresses succession planning, professionalizing operations, and managing family dynamics in family managed business environments
  • Comprehensive curriculum covering financial management, digital marketing, operations, and fund-raising
  • Personalized mentorship tailored to your specific business challenges
  • Explores best management practices, governance frameworks, and family continuity strategies for both multi-generational and first-generation entrepreneurs

01Who should apply for a family business management course?
This family business management course is ideal for next-generation business scions, active family enterprise members, budding entrepreneurs, and final-year students preparing to join their family business who want to professionalize operations and drive growth through an entrepreneurship development program.
02How is a family business management course different from an MBA?
A family business management course like PGPFMB focuses on building and scaling your own business with hands-on application in your enterprise, while MBAs offer broad corporate knowledge, making this family business management course uniquely practical with personalized mentorship for real business challenges.
03What is the duration of the PG in Entrepreneurship & Family Business Management program?
The family business management course is 11 months with one week monthly at Mumbai campus, three weeks implementing in your business, and one day for mentoring, ensuring this entrepreneurship course in India delivers practical impact while minimizing business disruption.
04What are the key benefits of this program?
This family business management program helps scale your business 2X, build automation systems, gain unicorn frameworks, expand your network, and address family business management challenges like succession planning - all with personalized mentorship and real-time application.
05What are the eligibility criteria for applying?
You must be a graduate from any recognized university and either own a business or be an active family business member to enroll in this entrepreneurship course in India.
06Are there any specific academic or professional prerequisites?
The family business management course requires a bachelor's degree from any discipline and ownership or active involvement in a family business, as this entrepreneurship management course AAA model requires practical application in your own enterprise during the entrepreneurship development program.
07Can I pursue this program part-time or online?
This program is full-time but business-owner friendly with one week monthly on campus and three weeks in your business. The family business management course isn't fully online as immersive networking and mentorship are integral to this post graduate diploma in entrepreneurship.
08What kind of mentorship support is provided?
Each participant receives personalized one-on-one mentorship throughout the program. Mentors are experienced business leaders who understand family business dynamics and provide guidance tailored to your specific industry and challenges.
09Are industrial visits mandatory?
No, industry visits are not mandatory but they are an integral part of the learning experience. These visits provide firsthand exposure to how leading family businesses and enterprises operate, offering practical insights you can apply to your own business.
10What happens if I miss a campus week?
Regular attendance is expected as each campus week builds on the previous one. However, we understand business emergencies occur. Contact the program coordinator immediately to discuss make-up options and ensure you don't fall behind.
11Can family members attend together?
Yes! We encourage multiple family members to participate as it creates alignment in vision and strategy. Special cohort discounts may be available for multiple enrollments from the same family business but it depends on the approval of the faculty.
12What is the typical class size?
We maintain small cohort sizes (typically 25-35 participants) to ensure personalized attention, meaningful peer interactions, and effective mentorship.
13Will this program help with business expansion and fundraising?
Absolutely. The curriculum includes dedicated modules on scaling operations, fund-raising strategies, investor pitching, and business expansion planning—all with practical application to your business.
