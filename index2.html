About the Program

Overview of Entrepreneurship & Family Business Management Course in India

AAA Learning Model 1 Week / Month on Campus Succession Planning Mumbai Campus

WeSchool's PGPFMB is a leading Family Managed Business program and a top-rated entrepreneurship course in Mumbai, designed for both next-generation leaders and first-generation entrepreneurs. This entrepreneurship course in India focuses on building strong foundations in management, governance, and continuity for sustainable business growth.

Structured around the AAA Model (Acquisition, Application & Assimilation), PGPFMB enables participants to directly implement learning within their Family Managed Business while continuing day-to-day operations. With just one week on campus each month, this program ensures a perfect balance between academic learning and practical execution.

What makes PGPFMB stand out is its focus on real-world challenges like succession planning, professionalization, and managing family dynamics. As a comprehensive entrepreneurship course in Mumbai and across India, it equips business owners with modern tools, mentorship, and global insights to scale and sustain their Family Managed Business effectively.

01What is an entrepreneurship development program?
An entrepreneurship development program is a structured initiative that equips individuals with skills to start, manage, and scale businesses, focusing on entrepreneurial thinking and practical application — making this entrepreneurship course in India different from traditional corporate-focused MBA programs.
02Who should apply for a family business management course?
This family business management course is ideal for next-generation business scions, active family enterprise members, budding entrepreneurs, and final-year students preparing to join their family business who want to professionalize operations and drive growth.
03How is an entrepreneurship course in India different from an MBA?
PGP-EFMB focuses on building and scaling your own business with hands-on application in your enterprise, while MBAs offer broad corporate knowledge. This family business management course is uniquely practical with personalized mentorship for real business challenges.
04Is this program a postgraduate diploma in entrepreneurship?
Yes, this is a post graduate diploma in entrepreneurship and family-managed business (PGP-EFMB) offered by WeSchool — an 11-month program that delivers a recognized diploma, transforms business capabilities, and provides access to extensive alumni networks and ongoing mentorship.
05What is the duration of the PG in Entrepreneurship & Family Business Management?
The family business management course is 11 months with one week monthly at Mumbai campus, three weeks implementing in your business, and one day for mentoring — ensuring practical impact while minimizing business disruption.
06What are the key benefits of this program?
Scale your business 2X, build automation systems, access Harvard and Babson learning, gain unicorn frameworks, expand your network, and address family business challenges like succession planning — all with personalized mentorship and real-time application.
07What are the eligibility criteria for applying?
You must be a graduate from any recognized university and either own a business or be an active family business member. Final-year students preparing to join their family enterprise can also apply for this post graduate diploma in entrepreneurship.
08What is the meaning of entrepreneurship management?
Entrepreneurship management applies management principles to entrepreneurial ventures and family businesses — covering strategic planning, innovation, and operational execution. It develops both the entrepreneurial mindset and managerial discipline needed to build sustainable, profitable enterprises.
09Are there specific academic or professional prerequisites?
The course requires a bachelor's degree from any discipline and ownership or active involvement in a family business. The AAA model requires practical application in your own enterprise throughout the program.
10Can I pursue this program part-time or online?
The program is full-time but business-owner friendly — one week on campus each month and three weeks in your own business. It is not fully online as immersive networking and mentorship are integral to this post graduate diploma in entrepreneurship.
